Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Remembot is a chat agent that answers from the live web and remembers what you asked. This page says plainly what we store, who else sees it, and how you remove it.

What we store

  • Your account: your name, your email address, and a salted PBKDF2 hash of your password. We never store the password itself and cannot read it.
  • Your chats: each question you ask, the answer, the web pages it cited, and the date and time. This is what lets Remembot remember. Only your account can read them.
  • Sign-in records: a hashed session token, and for 24 hours, failed sign-in attempts with the email or network address they came from, to stop password guessing.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your chats to train any model.

Cookies and local storage

  • One essential cookie, sid, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It is HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it, and it is only sent to this site. It needs no consent because the service cannot work without it.
  • Local storage in your browser remembers whether the sidebar is collapsed and your cookie choice. It never leaves your device.
  • Analytics, only if you choose “Accept all”: Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without tracking you across sites. You can change your choice any time with .

How long we keep it, and how to delete it

Chats stay until you delete them. To delete one chat, hover it in the sidebar and press the trash icon twice. To delete everything, open the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose Delete account: your account, every chat and every session are erased at once, permanently.

Security

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-SHA256 and a unique salt. Session tokens are random, stored only as hashes, and end when you sign out. Sign-in is rate-limited against guessing.

Your rights

You can see your data in the app, delete it at any time, and ask us about it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may also have the right to a copy of your data, to correct it, or to object to processing.

Children

Remembot is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact and changes

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before it takes effect.
