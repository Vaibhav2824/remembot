Questions

What is Remembot?

A chat agent with one tool, live web search, and a long memory. Every answer cites the pages it came from, and every question is saved with the date and time you asked it.

What does “ask again next week” mean?

Ask “What did I ask last Tuesday?” or “Has the pricing you found on Monday changed?” Remembot finds that exchange by its date, re-checks the facts against fresh sources, and tells you what changed.

Are my chats saved?

Yes. Every chat is saved to your account and listed in the sidebar, newest first, so you can reopen and continue any of them. You can delete a single chat, or your whole account, at any time.

Who can see my chats?

Only you. Your password is stored as a salted hash, never in plain text, and your session lives in a secure cookie that page scripts cannot read. See the Privacy Policy for the services that process your questions.

Does it cost anything?

No. Each account can ask up to 50 questions a day.
